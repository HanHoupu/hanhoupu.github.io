1. An AlphaZero-like diffusion-based Mahjong AI

The basic idea is simple: inspired by AlphaZero, we want to build a Mahjong AI. The goal is to make it as strong as possible, ideally close to SOTA.

However, this project has several difficult points. As far as I know, AlphaZero-style methods have not really been successfully applied to this kind of game.

  1. Mahjong is an imperfect-information game, so the AI needs to reason about what tiles the opponents may have.
  2. If we only look at our own hand, the complexity of Mahjong may be comparable to Go. If we also consider the discard pool and the possible hidden hands of other players, the complexity can become much larger.
  3. There are already Mahjong AIs, but many of them are based on human game records and feature learning. This is useful, but I think a more simulation-based and scalable approach would be interesting to explore.

My current thought is to use diffusion models because they may be suitable for discrete modeling, especially for modeling hidden information such as opponents’ hands.

Later, we may also build a full-information model as a teacher, and then distill it into a teacher-student network.

Because training data can be generated through simulation or self-play, there should be less manual data collection and cleaning. If we control the model size properly, I think it may be possible to train the model on a single RTX 3090 Ti or a similar GPU.

2. SFT for accelerating diffusion LLMs, especially with latent representations

If you follow open-source LLMs, you may know about LLaDA and Dream 8B. They are diffusion-based language models, not image diffusion models. Recently, Google also released an open-source diffusion LLM model.

One important feature of diffusion models is their strong parallelization potential. From image diffusion models, we know that diffusion can often be accelerated, so diffusion LLMs may also have room for speedup.

Another interesting idea comes from image diffusion: diffusion models can refine an output gradually, such as in super-resolution.

My early idea is: can we use a latent representation as an alternative to explicit CoT, or chain-of-thought? CoT can be viewed as a reasoning or optimization process, but converting hidden states into tokens may lose information. If we keep the reasoning process in a continuous high-dimensional hidden-state space, it may have stronger expressive power.

If possible, we may use one refined latent representation to generate a block of tokens in parallel, for example 256 tokens, instead of generating tokens one by one like a traditional autoregressive GPT model.

1. 类 AlphaZero 的 diffusion-based 麻将 AI

这个项目的出发点很简单：受 AlphaZero 的启发，我们写一个麻将版本的 AI，目标是尽可能接近 SOTA。

不过，这个项目有几个明显难点。至少在我的了解里，AlphaZero-style 方法还没有被很好地应用到这类游戏里。

  1. 麻将是不完全信息游戏，所以 AI 不只要看自己的手牌，还要推断对手可能有什么牌。
  2. 如果只看自己的手牌，麻将的复杂度可能和围棋接近；但如果把牌河和其他玩家的隐藏手牌也考虑进去，复杂度会大很多。
  3. 已有的一些麻将 AI 很多依赖人类棋谱和特征学习。这个方向当然有用，但我更想探索一种更偏模拟、自博弈、可扩展的做法。

我考虑使用 diffusion，是因为 diffusion 可能适合离散建模，尤其适合建模对手手牌这种隐藏信息。

后期我们也可以训练一个拥有全信息的 teacher model，再把它蒸馏到 student model 里。

由于训练数据可以通过模拟或者自博弈生成，所以应该不需要大量真实数据收集和清洗。如果模型规模控制得当，我认为一张 RTX 3090 Ti 或类似显卡也有机会跑起来。

2. diffusion LLM 的加速 SFT，尤其是 latent representation

如果你关注开源 LLM，可能知道 LLaDA 和 Dream 8B。它们是 diffusion-based language model，不是图像 diffusion 模型。最近，Google 也开源了一个 diffusion LLM 模型。

Diffusion 模型的一个重要特点是并行化潜力比较高。图像 diffusion 领域已经有很多加速思路，所以 diffusion LLM 可能也有加速空间。

另一个想法来自图像 diffusion：diffusion 可以不断细化输出，例如超分辨率。

我现在的初步想法是：能否用 latent representation 作为显式 CoT，也就是 chain-of-thought 的一种替代形式？CoT 可以看成一个推理或优化过程，但 hidden state 转成 token 的时候可能会损失一部分信息。如果我们把推理过程维持在连续的高维 hidden-state 空间里，可能会有更强的表达能力。

如果可行，我们甚至可以用同一个细化后的 latent representation 并行生成一段 token，比如 256 个 token，而不是像传统 autoregressive GPT 那样一个一个生成。
