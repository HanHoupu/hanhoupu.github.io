1. An AlphaZero-like diffusion-based Mahjong AI

The basic idea is simple: inspired by AlphaZero, we want to build a AI for Mahjong. The goal is to make it as strong as possible, ideally close to SOTA.

That being said, this project has several difficult points. As far as I know, AlphaZero-like algorithms have not really been successfully applied to this kind of game, or at least I have not read papers that directly do this.

  1. Mahjong is an imperfect-information game, so in terms of complexity, the AI needs to additionally reason about the hidden tiles held by the opponents.
  2. If we only look at our own hand, the complexity of Mahjong may be comparable to Go. But if we also consider the discard pool and the possible hidden hands of other players, the overall complexity may become around 10^100 times larger than Go in rough scale.
  3. There are already some Mahjong AIs, but many of them are based on human game records and feature learning. I think this approach is not elegant enough in terms of method and scalability. Our idea may look more interesting, but it also means there may not be much prior work to directly follow.

My current thought is to use diffusion models because diffusion may be useful as a conditional belief model for hidden information, especially for modeling hidden information such as opponents’ hands. The diffusion module is not meant to replace the policy/value model. It is mainly for hand-reading and belief modeling under partial observability.

Later, we may also build a full-information model as a teacher, and then distill it into a teacher-student network.

Since we do not need to collect real-world data, there should be less work on data cleaning. If we control the model size properly, I think it may even be possible to train the model on a single RTX 3090 Ti or a similar GPU.

If it's too difficult, we can downgrade the plan and simply use diffusion to build a Mahjong AI that is close to SOTA. We can collect high-quality game records and follow a more traditional RL method, without focusing on zero-human-data training like AlphaZero.

2. SFT for accelerating diffusion LLMs, especially with latent representations

If you follow open-source LLMs, you may know about LLaDA 8B and Dream 7B. They are diffusion-based language models, not image diffusion models. In June 2026, Google also open-sourced its diffusion LLM model.

One important feature of diffusion models is that they have strong parallelization potential. From image diffusion models and diffusion itself, we know that diffusion can often be accelerated, so diffusion LLMs may also have large room for speedup.

Another interesting idea comes from image diffusion: in the image domain, diffusion models can be used for tasks such as super-resolution, where the output is gradually refined.

My early idea is: can we use a latent representation to replace CoT, or chain-of-thought, and use it for better expression? Since CoT can be viewed as an optimization process, converting hidden states into tokens may be a collapse or dimensionality reduction step. If we can keep the reasoning process in a continuous high-dimensional hidden-state space, it may have stronger expressive power.

If possible, we may even use one refined latent representation to generate 256 tokens in parallel, achieving parallel acceleration, instead of waiting for tokens one by one like a traditional autoregressive GPT model.

If it's too difficult, we can also downscope the project to training-free acceleration methods for the generation stage of diffusion LLMs. SFT or testing a new training framework may indeed be too hard for one semester.

1. 类 AlphaZero 的 diffusion-based 麻将 AI

一句话来说，这个项目的出发点很简单：受 AlphaZero 的启发，我们写一个麻将版本的 AI，目标是最强 SOTA。

话是这么说，这个项目会有几个难点，以及AlphaZero类似的算法从来没有在类似游戏中实现过的，最起码我没有读过类似的论文。

  1. 麻将是一个不完全信息类游戏，所以在复杂度上，我们要额外去猜对手遮起来的牌的复杂度。
  2. 如果只看自己的牌，麻将的复杂度和围棋旗鼓相当；但如果考虑牌河和对家手牌的可能性，那么整体复杂度将会是围棋的 10^100 倍。
  3. 市面上确实有类似的麻将 AI，不过都是走人类棋谱 + 特征学习，在方法和可扩展性上我认为不够优美。所以我们这个虽然看上去好看，但是没什么前人的参考。

我来解释一下我的具体想法：使用 diffusion 是因为 diffusion 可能适合被用来建模麻将中的隐藏信息，尤其是在针对对手手牌进行读牌的领域，它可能非常适合。diffusion 模块不是为了替代 policy/value model，而是主要用于不完全信息下的读牌和 belief modeling。

我们可能会希望后期出现拥有全信息的模型作为 teacher，然后对它进行蒸馏，形成一个 teacher-student 的网络。

由于我们不需要进行任何数据收集，所以数据清洗之类的杂活不用干；且如果规模控制得当的话，我认为在一张 3090 Ti 上（或者类似的卡）进行训练都是有可能的。

以上想法我们实在不行可以降级考虑用diffusion做一个跟sota差不多的就行了，抓优质的对局走传统RL路线，没有必要非要惦记zero的无人工数据训练

2. diffusion LLM 的加速 SFT，尤其是 latent

如果对开源 LLM 有关注的话，各位可能知道之前的 LLaDA 8B 和 Dream 7B 模型。它们是基于 diffusion 的大语言模型，而不是画图模型。2026/6，谷歌也发布开源了它们的 diffusion LLM 模型。

Diffusion 模型的一个很大的特点就是可并行性很高。也就是说，从图像 diffusion / diffusion 本身的特性来看，它的加速潜力非常大。

第二个有趣的事情是，在 diffusion 的图像领域中，我们可以使用 diffusion 进行一些超分辨率的解析，即不断进行细化。

那么现在我的一个初步想法是：我们能否使用一个 latent 来代替 CoT，即思维链，来起到更好的表达？因为 CoT 可以看成一个优化过程，所以从 hidden state 到 token 本身是一个坍缩 / 降维的过程；如果我们能一直维持连续的高维 hidden state 形态，可能会有更强的表达能力。

甚至可能的话，我们可以用同一个细化好的 latent 来进行 256 个 token 的同时输出，来达成并行加速的效果，而不是像传统的 AR GPT 一样需要一个一个等待 token。

以上想法我们实在不行可以降级考虑进行一些trainning-free的DLLM的生成阶段的加速工作，SFT或者试新框架可能确实过难
